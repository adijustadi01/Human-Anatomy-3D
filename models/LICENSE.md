# Licence for the 3D models in this folder

The files in this folder (`*.js`, except this note) are compressed 3D surface meshes derived from

> **BodyParts3D**, © The Database Center for Life Science,
> licensed under CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan.
> https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/ · https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/deed.en

**Changes made:** each mesh was converted from STL, rescaled to metres, simplified to a lower
triangle count, quantised and compressed, assigned a display colour, and grouped into one pack
per body region by `tools/build-3d/build-models.js`. The shapes themselves were not edited.

Because the source licence is share-alike, these derived files are released under the same
licence: **Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan**. If you redistribute or adapt
them, keep the credit above and use the same licence.

This licence applies to the model data only, not to the rest of the app.
